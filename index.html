It’s been the job. I am a retired firefighter with 34 years of service, including 20 years with Fort Hood Fire & Emergency Services. I also proudly served in the United States Coast Guard as a firefighter and Damage Controlman, serving overseas and aboard several large Coast Guard cutters. My career has been built around protecting people, solving problems under pressure, and taking responsibility when the stakes are high. I have served our country nationally and our community here in Copperas Cove as a firefighter and emergency responder. I assisted in disaster-response efforts during Hurricane Harvey in Houston and during major flooding events across Central Texas. I remain active in my local VFW and previously served as Post Commander of American Legion Post 582 in Copperas Cove. A RECORD OF SERVICE Prepared when it matters most. 01 Fire & Emergency Services 34 years in fire and emergency response, with two decades serving at Fort Hood Fire & Emergency Services. 02 U.S. Coast Guard Veteran Served as a firefighter and Damage Controlman, including overseas duty and service aboard large Coast Guard cutters. 03 Disaster Response Responded to Hurricane Harvey in Houston and supported flood-response operations across Central Texas. 04 Veterans Leadership Active in the local VFW and a past Post Commander of American Legion Post 582 in Copperas Cove. HOW I LEAD Straightforward. Fair. Accountable. I believe public service should work the same way emergency service does: identify the problem, tell people the truth, make a plan, and finish the job. Accountability Wrongdoing should be addressed, not ignored because it is inconvenient or politically uncomfortable. Follow-Through When I accept responsibility for a task, I expect it to be completed and completed well. Fairness I am direct and I will ask hard questions, but I believe everyone deserves to be treated fairly. Service Before Politics The job is about the people of Copperas Cove and practical results for the community.
